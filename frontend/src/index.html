Import question bank

Choose one or more Module N Question Pool.pdf files, or a JSON bank. Questions are matched by module and number (e.g. M1-Q9), so importing again updates the text but keeps your answer history.

Anything that can't be parsed cleanly, such as a missing answer or an answer that isn't one of the choices, is listed below and skipped, not guessed at.

Reset stats

Delete your whole answer history: every attempt, streak and accuracy figure. The questions stay, so you can start drilling again from zero.

Remove modules

Removing a module deletes its questions, figures and answer history. You can bring the questions back by importing the PDF again, but the history is gone for good.

Hard reset

Delete everything: all modules, questions, figures and answer history. The app will be as it was on first launch.
